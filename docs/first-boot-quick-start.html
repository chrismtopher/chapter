Join Setup Wi-Fi

On your phone or computer, connect to Chapter-Setup.

Open The Address

In a browser, enter the address shown on the player screen.

Add Home Wi-Fi

Enter your Wi-Fi name and password, then submit.

Add Audiobookshelf

Enter the server address and player account login.

Browse Knob

Turn to move. Click to choose. Hold 3 seconds for Bluetooth.

Volume Knob

Turn for volume. Click to mute. Hold 10 seconds for setup address.

Tip: If setup is needed later, hold the volume knob for 10 seconds.
After setup, the player shows your library or playback screen.
